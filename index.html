GOOD TO KNOW

A few common
questions.

Are domain and hosting included? +

No. Domain registration and hosting are separate third-party costs unless a quotation explicitly includes them. You should retain ownership of your accounts.

Do I need to provide the content? +

Yes, for standard packages you provide the text, logo and images. Content writing or substantial rewriting can be quoted as an add-on.

Can you publish the website on GitHub Pages or cPanel? +

Yes, for suitable static websites. The best hosting option depends on the website’s features; backend systems, databases and server-side functionality need a different hosting plan.

Will my website rank first on Google? +

No ethical developer can promise a specific ranking. Basic SEO setup improves foundational signals, but rankings depend on competition, content, authority, user needs and other factors over time.

What happens if I need changes after launch? +

The included revision rounds and post-launch defect support will be written in your quotation. New features and ongoing updates are charged separately or covered by a maintenance agreement.
